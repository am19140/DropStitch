Privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

DropStitch is a knitting app that reads your patterns and counts your rows. We keep what we collect to a minimum, and we never sell your data or show you ads.

Your knitting

Your projects, patterns (PDFs and photos), notes, row counts, timers and yarn stash are stored on your device.

If you sign in, they are also saved to your account so they're safe if you lose your phone and the same on every phone you use. Only you can reach them: each account can only read its own knitting and pattern files, and the files are kept private. When you sign out, they're removed from that phone and stay in your account.

If you don't sign in, nothing leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes it all.

Your account

Signing in is optional. If you create an account, we keep:

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send account emails such as confirming your address or resetting your password.
  • Your password, stored only as a secure one-way hash by our sign-in provider. We can never see it.
  • If you continue with Google: your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account, which Google shares with us when you agree to it. We don't get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on this website, we keep your email address and, if you tell us, whether you use an iPhone or an Android phone. We use them only to invite you to try DropStitch early, a few knitters at a time. No newsletters. Write to hello@dropstitch.io and we'll take you off the list. We delete the waitlist once DropStitch is in the app stores.

Who helps us run DropStitch

  • Supabase runs our sign-in service and stores your account, the knitting saved to it, and the waitlist.
  • Resend sends our emails, such as confirming your address or resetting your password.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website.

They only process data to provide their service to us.

Your choices

You can use DropStitch without an account. You can delete your account and everything saved in it at any time in the app: You → Delete account. It's removed straight away, for good. You can also write to hello@dropstitch.io to see, correct or delete your data, and we'll do it within 30 days.

Children

DropStitch isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is important.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Write to hello@dropstitch.io.
