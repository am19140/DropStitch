Cosy
Raglan

A top-down sweater in five sizes. Nine pages. One of them is yours.

Read all instructions before you begin. Some steps happen at the same time.

Cosy Raglan8

Chart A

Optional colourwork yoke. Work chart A over the last 18 rnds of the yoke, beg with rnd 1 at BOR. Read all rnds from right to left. Rep the 8-st rep 33 (35, 38, 41, 43) times around.

□ knit in MC   ■ knit in CC   ◪ k2tog in CC

Carry the yarn not in use loosely across the back of the work. Catch floats longer than 5 sts every 3rd st. On rnd 14 only, work the dec sts as shown for sizes S and L; all other sizes knit these sts in MC.

Sizes XS and M only: omit the last 2 rnds of the chart and work 2 rnds in MC instead.

Gauge

22 sts and 30 rows = 10 cm in st st on 4 mm needles, after blocking. Change needle size if necessary to obtain the correct gauge.

Abbreviations

k knit · p purl · k2tog knit 2 together · ssk slip, slip, knit · M1L make 1 left · M1R make 1 right · pm place marker · sm slip marker · BOR beginning of round · w&t wrap and turn · rep repeat · foll following · rem remaining · patt pattern

Notes

The yoke is worked in the round from the neck down. Numbers for the other sizes are given in brackets. Where only one number is given, it applies to all sizes. A 0 means nothing is worked for that size.

Materials

5 (5, 6, 6, 7) skeins of DK wool, approx. 225 m per 100 g. 3.5 mm and 4 mm circular needles, 40 and 80 cm. 3.5 mm and 4 mm dpns. 4 stitch markers, 1 in a contrasting colour for BOR. Waste yarn, tapestry needle.

Ease

Designed with 8–12 cm of positive ease. Sample shown in size S on a 86 cm bust. For a closer fit, choose the size below your bust measurement.

3Yoke

Collar

With 3.5 mm circular needle, cast on 88 (92, 96, 104, 108) sts. Join to work in the rnd, being careful not to twist, and pm for BOR. Work in k1, p1 rib for 6 (6, 7, 7, 8) cm.

Set-up

Change to 4 mm needle. Next rnd: K14 (14, 15, 16, 17) for right sleeve, pm, k30 (32, 33, 36, 37) for front, pm, k14 (14, 15, 16, 17) for left sleeve, pm, k30 (32, 33, 36, 37) for back.

Raglan increases

Rnd 1 (inc): *K1, M1L, knit to 1 st before m, M1R, k1, sm; rep from * 3 more times. 8 sts inc. Rnd 2: Knit. Rep rnds 1–2 a further 21 (23, 25, 27, 29) times. 264 (284, 304, 328, 348) sts.

Sizes L and XL only: work rnd 1 every rnd 2 (4) more times, then cont as set.

Try on as you go: the yoke should reach the underarm when worked to the measurement below. If it is short, work 1 (1, 2, 2, 2) more inc rnds before dividing.

Yoke depth from end of rib: 19 (20, 21, 22, 23.5) cm. Place a removable marker in the first inc rnd to measure from.

Cosy Raglan4

AT THE SAME TIME, when yoke meas 8 (8, 9, 9, 10) cm from end of rib, work short rows across the back as foll:

Short row 1 (RS): Knit to 6 sts before BOR m, w&t. Short row 2 (WS): Purl to 6 sts past the 3rd m, w&t. Short row 3: Knit to 4 sts before wrapped st, w&t. Short row 4: Purl to 4 sts before wrapped st, w&t. Rep short rows 3–4 once (once, twice, twice, 3 times) more.

Note: do not work raglan increases on short rows. Pick up wraps on the next rnd. See tutorial on page 9.

Divide for body and sleeves

Next rnd: *Remove m, place 58 (62, 66, 72, 76) sleeve sts on waste yarn, cast on 6 (6, 8, 8, 10) sts, sm, knit to next m; rep from * once more. 160 (172, 188, 204, 216) body sts.

Place a new BOR m at the centre of the left underarm cast-on sts, and a side m at the centre of the right underarm cast-on sts.

Sizes M, L and XL only: knit 1 rnd, then work 1 more set of short rows as before, beg 2 sts closer to BOR.

5Body

Cont in st st until body meas 4 cm from underarm. Dec rnd: *K1, k2tog, knit to 3 sts before m, ssk, k1, sm; rep from * once more. 4 sts dec. Rep dec rnd every 10th (10th, 12th, 12th, 14th) rnd 3 more times.

Work even until body meas 30 (31, 32, 33, 34) cm from underarm, or 5 cm shorter than desired length, ending 1 rnd before BOR.

Hem

Change to 3.5 mm needle. Work in k1, p1 rib for 5 cm. Bind off loosely in patt.

If your row gauge differs, see Adjusting length, page 8.

Side slits (optional)

Work hem back and forth in two halves: k1, p1 rib across front sts only for 5 cm, slipping the first st of every row. Rejoin yarn to back sts and work as for front, then work 2 rows more. Bind off both halves in patt.

AT THE SAME TIME, for a longer back, work 6 (6, 8, 8, 10) short rows across the back before beg hem.

Cosy Raglan6

Sleeves

Return 58 (62, 66, 72, 76) held sleeve sts to 4 mm dpns. Beg at centre of underarm, pick up and knit 3 (3, 4, 4, 5) sts, knit sleeve sts, pick up and knit 3 (3, 4, 4, 5) sts. Pm for BOR. 64 (68, 74, 80, 86) sts.

Knit 10 rnds. Dec rnd: K1, k2tog, knit to last 3 sts, ssk, k1. Rep dec rnd every 8th (8th, 7th, 7th, 6th) rnd 9 (10, 11, 13, 14) more times. 44 (46, 50, 52, 56) sts.

Cont even until sleeve meas 40 (41, 42, 43, 44) cm from underarm. Work cuff as for hem.

Work second sleeve the same, reversing shapings.

Balloon sleeve variation

Omit all dec rnds. When sleeve meas 32 (33, 34, 35, 36) cm, work k2tog to end. 32 (34, 37, 40, 43) sts. Sizes S and L only: k2tog once more at end of rnd. Work cuff in rib for 8 cm.

Note that the balloon sleeve takes approx. 30 m more yarn per sleeve.

7Finishing

Finishing

Weave in all ends. Close any holes at the underarms. Wet block to the measurements given in the schematic on page 2. Lay flat to dry.

Optional pocket

With 4 mm needle, cast on 24 (24, 26, 26, 28) sts and work in st st for 12 cm. Work 4 rows in rib. Bind off. Sew to front, 6 (6, 7, 7, 8) cm above hem, centred.

Errata: on page 5, the dec rnd should be worked every 10th (not 12th) rnd for size S.

Neckband (alternative)

For a higher neck, pick up and knit 1 st in each cast-on st around collar, and work in k1, p1 rib for a further 4 (4, 5, 5, 6) cm. Fold to the inside and sew down loosely.

Care

Hand wash cool. Dry flat. Do not tumble dry. Store folded.
